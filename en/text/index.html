FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Text Tools

Is the text I enter sent to a server?

No, all counting and text processing runs directly in your browser — no text is ever sent to any ToolkitMe server.

Is there a limit on text length?

There is no hard limit set by ToolkitMe, only the limits of your browser’s capability for extremely long text.

Does the word count here match Microsoft Word?

The counting method is similar (words are split by whitespace), though results can differ slightly depending on special characters in the text.
