FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Compress PNG

Does PNG transparency survive compression?

Yes, ToolkitMe’s PNG compression preserves the original image’s alpha (transparency) channel.

Why doesn’t the compressed PNG shrink by much sometimes?

PNG uses lossless compression with different limits than JPG, so the savings are usually smaller than JPG — especially for images with lots of color gradients.

Is it safe for images with sensitive data like screenshots?

Yes, since all compression happens in your own browser, images are never sent to any ToolkitMe server.
