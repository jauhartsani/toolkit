FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about the Story Downloader

Can I download a Story from any account?

Yes, as long as the account is public and the Story is still active within 24 hours of being posted.

Does the account owner know their Story was downloaded?

ToolkitMe only fetches the public link you provide and doesn’t interact with the related Instagram account in any way.

What if the link has already expired?

If the Story is past 24 hours and has been automatically removed by Instagram, the link can no longer be processed.
