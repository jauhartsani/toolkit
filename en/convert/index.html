FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Convert Image

What formats are supported?

This tool supports converting between JPG, PNG, and WebP, both as the source and target format.

Does image quality drop when converting?

For JPG and WebP targets, there’s an adjustable quality slider. For PNG, the conversion is lossless, so visual quality stays intact.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No, conversion uses the browser’s built-in Canvas API, so images never leave your device.
