Frequently Asked Questions about the Calculator

Does this BMI calculator replace medical advice?

No. ToolkitMe’s BMI calculator only gives a general estimate based on the WHO formula (weight/height²), not a substitute for a checkup or advice from a healthcare professional.

Is the data I enter stored?

No, all calculations run directly in your browser and are never sent to or stored on any ToolkitMe server.

How is the discount calculator formula worked out?

Price after discount = Original Price − (Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100).
