FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about the WhatsApp QR

What is the correct WhatsApp number format?

Use the country code without a plus sign or leading 0 — for example, an Indonesian number 0812xxxxxxx is written as 62812xxxxxxx.

Is the auto-message required?

No. If left empty, the QR code will simply open a WhatsApp chat to that number with no pre-filled message.

Can this QR be scanned from WhatsApp as well as a regular camera?

Yes. The QR points to a standard wa.me link, so it can be opened via phone camera, any QR scanner app, or WhatsApp’s own scan feature.

Is my WhatsApp number stored by ToolkitMe?

No, the entire QR and wa.me link generation process runs in your browser — no data is sent to or stored on any ToolkitMe server.
