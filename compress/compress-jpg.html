Frequently Asked Questions

Does compressing a JPG reduce its quality?
A small amount of quality loss is normal with JPG compression, but this tool lets you preview and adjust the quality level so you keep a good balance between file size and sharpness.
Is my photo uploaded to a server?
No. The JPG compress tool runs 100% in your browser using JavaScript — your file never leaves your device.
What is the maximum file size I can compress?
There's no hard limit enforced by the tool itself; performance depends on your device's memory since everything is processed locally.
