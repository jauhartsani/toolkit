Frequently Asked Questions

Will converting JPG to PNG improve quality?
PNG is lossless, but converting an already-compressed JPG won't recover detail lost during the original JPG compression.
Does PNG support transparency after conversion?
A JPG has no transparency data, so the converted PNG will have a solid background.
