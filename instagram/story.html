Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I download any username's story directly?
Instagram Stories require a logged-in session to view, so this tool only works with a Story link that has been directly and publicly shared, not by searching a username.
Does this notify the story owner?
This tool does not interact with Instagram's app in a way that triggers view notifications, since it only reads a public link you provide.
What if the link doesn't work?
The Story may have expired (Stories disappear after 24 hours) or the account may be private.
