Frequently Asked Questions

Is my WiFi password sent to a server?
No, the QR code is generated locally in your browser — your password never leaves your device.
Which devices can scan a WiFi QR code?
Most modern Android and iPhone camera apps can scan and connect automatically.
What if my network has no password?
Select the 'No Password' security option when generating the code.
