Frequently Asked Questions

Does the QR code expire?
No, a URL QR code works as long as the destination link stays active — there's no expiry set by this tool.
Can I track scans on this QR code?
This generator creates a static QR code without built-in scan tracking.
What format is the downloaded QR code?
It downloads as a PNG image, ready to print or share digitally.
